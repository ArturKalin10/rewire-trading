Don't try to win every trade. Try to survive every day. The traders still standing are the ones who get to compound.

Mindset

Two ways to trade a day.

Win-focused

Every trade has to be a winner. A loss feels personal, size creeps up, and one bad day can end the account.

Survival-first

Every trade has a defined, acceptable loss. A small loss is the cost of doing business, and a bad day stays a bad day.

Method

Tactics for the market. Training for the mind.

Protect capital

Set a daily loss limit before the open, size every position to it, and stop when it is reached. Survival is a rule, not a hope.

Regulate your state

Learn how stress narrows attention and pushes impulsive choices, then use simple routines to reset before you click.

Review to rewire

Score your process, not your profit. Consistent review builds new habits until disciplined trading becomes the default.
